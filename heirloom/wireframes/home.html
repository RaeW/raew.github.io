The re-engagement strip is the first thing you see — not your recipe list. The app opens with a reason to cook, not a catalogue to browse.
Ingredient search lives on the home screen as a persistent entry point, not inside the library. You don't have to know what you want to cook to start.
"Never cooked yet" is a first-class category. Recipes you imported but haven't made stay visible until you cook them.
"Needs attention" surfaces flagged imports without burying them. They stay visible until resolved.
