I'm obsessed by the messy space where real-world work meets software systems.

Over the course of my career, I've worked as both a developer and inside operational environments that depend heavily on software tools. That experience has made me especially attentive to workflow gaps, hidden system assumptions, and the small design decisions that shape how people actually work.

These case studies are working documents — the thinking is visible, including where it changed.
